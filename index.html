Research Paper · Under review

Tutor quality is not tutor-student fit: Evidence from online secondary-school tutoring

Karan Gupta and Arhaan Anand · Journal of Education and Learning

Tutoring platforms screen tutors on quality, yet families often say a tutoring relationship failed because the tutor did not suit the student. This study tests whether tutor quality and tutor-student fit are really different things, using records from 220 tutoring relationships on an online IGCSE platform and separate student and parent questionnaires.

They are. Quality and fit were almost uncorrelated, and both independently predicted student satisfaction, with fit carrying the larger weight. Quality varied mainly between tutors, while fit varied within each tutor's own set of students. The practical conclusion: screening tutors for quality cannot replace matching them well. On the parent side, perceived value and platform responsiveness were each linked to satisfaction, while the session fee was not.

I provided the study setting and administrative records and contributed to the design of the questionnaires. Data were collected in May and June 2026 under formal ethics approval.
